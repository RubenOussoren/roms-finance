## Overall judgment and confidence

ROMS Finance has a coherent Rails monolith and substantial financial-domain test infrastructure. Preserve the model-oriented design, calculator boundary, specialized simulators, and Hotwire UI. The next investment should make authorization, simulation lifecycle, and deployment guarantees explicit and measurable, not replace Rails, introduce microservices, or move all business logic into services.

- **High confidence:** the cited structural/configuration facts and the existence of the inspected test cases. Line references below are baseline-specific, not promises about future HEADs.
- **Moderate confidence:** the prioritization of maintenance work from those facts. Runtime frequency, blast radius, and effort need owner validation.
- **Low confidence / unverified:** current suite health, actual coverage percentages, production configuration, restore readiness, performance bottlenecks, and exploitable tenant leaks. Source inspection alone cannot establish these.

## Operability: existing foundation, incomplete assurance

The multi-stage production image precompiles assets and runs non-root (`Dockerfile:22–59`). The self-host example has persistent app/DB/Redis volumes and web/database/Redis health checks (`compose.example.yml:73–147`). Hosting docs include proxy guidance, logs, backup/restore commands and destructive-volume warnings (`docs/hosting/docker.md:141–192`). Those are strengths to extend, not replace.

A database dump alone does not demonstrate complete recovery of stored files, configuration/encryption material and compatible application/schema versions. The cited guide provides commands, not a dated full restore drill or recovery-time/data-loss objectives. Actual backup scheduling, secrets handling, monitoring/alert delivery, job backlog recovery and live deployment rollback remain outside this inspection. Distinguish the tracked development sandbox in `compose.dev.yml:1,56–75` from the self-host deployment example; its configurable bind defaults to loopback and its environment is development, not a public production profile. Machine-local `compose.yml` overrides are not baseline repository evidence.
